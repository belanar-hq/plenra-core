# Mosquito Pilot PASS/HOLD/BLOCK Examples

## PASS

- Localized recurring mosquito problem in one home or building area
- Clear operational scope, such as yard, balcony, stairwell, or apartment
- Reachable area for a human-supervised pilot check
- Realistic expectation: review the case and decide the next manual step

## HOLD

- Unclear location or missing neighborhood
- Contradictory details, such as two different addresses or problem areas
- Unclear severity or timing
- Missing operational details needed before scheduling or follow-up

## BLOCK

- Unsafe requests or requests requiring non-pilot handling
- Outside supported region
- Impossible guarantees requested
- Unrelated pest requests
