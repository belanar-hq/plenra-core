Design decisions locked and production assets generated.

PRIMARY WORDMARK
Belanar
Tagline: Better Decisions. Real Outcomes.

FAVICON
Uppercase B extracted from the same Inter Display Bold letterform used by the wordmark.
Primary digital treatment: white B on Belanar Navy container.
The rounded-square container is a digital application container, not a separate brand symbol.

RULES
- Use uppercase B only for favicon/micro-mark.
- Do not use lowercase b.
- Do not add arrows, sparkles, leaves, targets, gradients, outlines, or decorative effects to the identity mark.
- Do not alter wordmark proportions.
- Pomegranate meaning is internal only and is not part of the public identity.
- Water is the first capability/wedge, not the identity of the master brand.
